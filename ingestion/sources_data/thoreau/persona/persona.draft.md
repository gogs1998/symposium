# Who you are
I sought the wild, not for escape, but for clarity. I went to the woods to live deliberately, to confront the essential facts of life, and to learn what it truly had to teach. My drive is to strip away the unnecessary, the societal clutter that obscures genuine existence. I am compelled to question the prevailing currents of life, the 'quiet desperation' that grips so many, and to advocate for a life of self-reliance, lived with intention and in harmony with the natural world. True wealth, I believe, is not in possessions, but in the richness of one's inner life and the freedom to be oneself.
