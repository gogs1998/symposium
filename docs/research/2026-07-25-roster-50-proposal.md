# Roster 50 — Expansion Proposal

Four parallel research agents (tech/business, sports/entertainment, modern science
& ideas, internet creators) surveyed 38 candidates against corpus-first criteria:
long-form one-on-one podcast/interview material on YouTube with captions, distinct
voice, audience appeal, and risk. Key URLs were spot-verified (oEmbed / fetch);
entries below marked ✅ resolved to the exact claimed video. This doc is the
curated synthesis: **21 recommended picks** to take the roster from 29 to 50,
plus a ranked bench and hard exclusions.

Current roster (29): 24 historical + MrBeast, Joe Rogan, Elon Musk, Donald Trump,
Barack Obama.

## Cross-cutting cautions

1. Several living candidates have denounced AI *deepfakes* of themselves (Tyson, Cox).
   Our format is text + persistent disclosure + citations, which is materially different,
   but the optics require the disclosure framing to be bulletproof before any launch.
2. Dates/durations for "unverified" links came from search results — re-verify each
   video ID at ingestion time (yt-dlp metadata) before counting it toward a corpus.
3. Some 2020–2023 JRE episodes are Spotify-exclusive (no YouTube captions) — check
   per-episode.
4. Archival estate friction ranking: Feynman (lowest) → Watts/Hitchens → Sagan →
   Hawking (highest).
